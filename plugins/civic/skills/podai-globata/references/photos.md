# Photos: keeping time and location

Phones store time and GPS inside the photo (Exif) only if the camera has location access, and many apps strip it
on upload. Say this kindly, once, and give the matching tip:

- **Camera**: location for the camera app must be on (iPhone: Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Camera → While
  Using; Android: the camera app's settings → "Save location" / "Запазване на местоположението").
- **iPhone → Claude app**: if location is missing, share from the Photos app instead (Share → Options at the top →
  turn on Location → Claude), or attach through Files.
- **Android → Claude app**: the system photo picker removes location from photos it hands to apps. Attach the photos
  as files ("Файлове" / "Files" → Pictures/DCIM) instead of picking from the gallery, or share from Google Photos or
  the gallery app → Claude.
- **Messengers** (Viber, WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram) strip metadata. Use the originals from the phone.
- Screenshots have no GPS.

No location at all is fine: ask for the street and number or the intersection, or a map pin. In Google Maps,
long-press the spot and the coordinates are copied; paste them into the chat.

Good photos for a report: one wide shot showing the car and what it blocks (crossing, ramp, sign, sidewalk),
one close-up where the plate is readable. Night photos: avoid flash glare on the plate.
